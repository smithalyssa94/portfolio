McCrea's Candies

The Problem

McCrea's makes exceptional caramels — small batch, real ingredients, the kind of product that sells itself once someone tastes it. But the brand didn't match the product.

The client had a warehouse full of simple, solid packaging waiting to be filled, so our challenge was to reinvent the brand without touching the packaging. The entire brand refresh had to happen through digital presence and marketing.

The Rundown

  • Creative direction
  • Competitive audit
  • Brand strategy and positioning
  • Manifesto and personality framework
  • Voice and messaging
  • Email marketing strategy and copy
  • Seasonal campaign (social + paid)
  • Website architecture

The Thinking

Caramel isn't a reach-for-it-every-day candy. You need a reason. The brand had to create that reason by connecting taste to memory — specific flavors tied to specific moments that deserve slowing down. The language got rich and evocative, the style got warm and nostalgic, and the whole brand started operating at the intersection of scent, taste, and emotion. We were developing moments worth savoring, and giving people a reason to have one.

The Work

Brand Manifesto and Positioning

When revamping the brand, we wanted to better reflect the warm, colorful, kinda kooky personality of the team behind the candy. By invoking Martha Stewart and Indiana Jones as personality figureheads, we were marrying McCrea's sentimental, thoughtful side with the adventurous, unexpected nature of their candy flavors.

Ecommerce Email System

The emails had to carry the brand voice into someone's inbox without sounding like marketing. Emails and marketing connected caramels to a specific flavor and invoked a specific moment— seasonal, sensory, personal. The system was behavioral and seasonal, not batch-and-blast, so the voice showed up at the right time in the right context.

Holiday Campaign

McCrea's had never run a seasonal campaign. The caramel advent calendar became the anchor — a product that's already about daily ritual and anticipation. Strategy, customer journey, funnel, social, and paid, all built from scratch against a tight budget.

The Result

2x Best sales day on record — previous high within 12 months
2.3x ROAS on the holiday advent calendar campaign
